How the NRS Service Surveys work

A plain-language guide for NRS staff

November 2026  ·  Version 1

At a glance

There are two online forms. Each one has its own web address and its own QR code. Both save their answers automatically, so nobody has to type results into a spreadsheet.

Section 1 — At a glance

Taxpayer Satisfaction Survey

For taxpayers. Scan the QR code at the tax office, or open the link.

  • Takes about 5 minutes.
  • Confidential.
  • No name needed. Tax ID and contact details are optional.

Service Optimization Checklist

For NRS staff, in three roles:

  • Officers at the unit fill it in.
  • CSQAU scorers review it and give scores.
  • The NRS team follows progress.

The Taxpayer Survey

This is what a taxpayer sees and answers. It is short, friendly and works on any phone.

Section 2 — The Taxpayer Satisfaction Survey
  1. Tax office. Pick the State, then the Office where you were served.
  2. Section A: Taxpayer / business information. Optional.
  3. Section B: Tax compliance.
  4. Section C: Service experience. How was your visit?
  5. Section D: Digital channels and awareness. Which NRS channels do you know and use?
  6. Section E: Your feedback. Anything else you would like to tell us.

Optional parts. Section A (including the Tax ID) and contact details can be left blank. Nobody has to give a name.

The top of the live survey.

Our promise to taxpayers: answers are confidential. They are used only to improve service and are handled in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.
Where the answers go

When the taxpayer presses Submit, the answers go straight into a Google Sheet called “NRS Taxpayer Satisfaction Survey - Responses”. Each response is one row. The sheet is the report: it updates by itself.

Access to the sheet is by invitation. Taxpayers cannot see it, and the survey page never shows anyone else's answers.

Tip for field teams: if a taxpayer prefers, hand over your phone or point to the poster. The same link works for everyone. Please do not fill it in on their behalf.

Step 1: officers

The Service Optimization Checklist is filled in at the unit by the officer on duty. It takes a few minutes.

Section 3 — Checklist, Step 1 (officers)
  1. Scan the QR code or open nrssurvey.dako.studio/staff.
  2. Choose the State, then the Office. Check that it is your own office.
  3. Answer Yes or No for each of the 10 sections, and add remarks where they help.
  4. Add the staff details at the bottom of the form.
  5. Press Submit. You will see a reference such as NRS-1A2B3C4D. Write it down or take a screenshot.
  6. Your answers now wait for scoring. Officers do not see any scores.

The top of the live checklist. The dark button, “CSQAU scorer? Open the scoring page”, is only for scorers.

Step 2: CSQAU scorers

Scoring is done by the visiting Customer Service Quality Assurance Unit (CSQAU) checker, never by the officer.

Section 4 — Checklist, Step 2 (scoring)
  1. On the checklist page, press “CSQAU scorer? Open the scoring page”. (Or open nrssurvey.dako.studio/staff/score.)
  2. Type the passcode and press Unlock.
  3. Pick the State, the Office, then a waiting submission.
  4. The officer's answers show on screen. You can read them but not change them.
  5. Enter a score from 0 to 100 for each section.
  6. Fill in Checked by, Head and Tax Controller details.
  7. Press Save. The system works out the total itself.

The live scoring page asks for the passcode first.

When something goes wrong

  • Sheet update failed? The finished record goes to the staff Google Sheet. If that step fails you will see the message “Saved, but the sheet update failed” and a Resend to sheet button. Your scores are safe: press it to try again.
  • Wrong passcode? After 5 wrong passcodes, that connection is locked out for 15 minutes.

Where things are saved

Nothing is stored on your phone or computer. Everything is saved online, as soon as you press Submit or Save.

Section 6 — Where things are saved

Waiting and scored

  • Waiting. The officer has submitted. Nobody has scored it yet. It is kept safely in the secure database.
  • Scored. A CSQAU scorer has saved the scores. The finished record is kept in the secure database as a backup and added as a finished row in the staff Google Sheet.

The reference code

Each submission gets a reference such as NRS-1A2B3C4D. It follows the submission everywhere: on the scoring page, in the secure database and as a column in the staff Google Sheet. Quote it if you need help.

The Google Sheets are the reports

NRS uses the two Google Sheets as its reports. They are read by invitation only. The secure database is the safety copy behind the scenes: you never need to open it.

Why two copies? If the sheet is briefly unavailable, nothing is lost. The scores are in the secure database, and the scorer can press Resend later.

Questions and fixes

I am locked out for 15 minutes

That happens after 5 wrong passcodes. Wait 15 minutes, then type the passcode carefully. Do not ask others for the passcode; ask the CSQAU lead.

I picked the wrong office

Tell the CSQAU lead the reference code. Do not submit a second form. The scorer can check which submission is the right one.

I scored the wrong submission

Stop and tell the CSQAU lead straight away, with the reference code. Scores cannot be changed on the page once saved, so the team needs to correct it.

It says someone else already scored this submission

This “scored twice” message means scores were already saved for this reference. Nothing is overwritten. Choose another waiting submission, or check with your colleague.

“Sheet update failed” and the Resend button

Your scores are safe. Press Resend to sheet. If it still fails, wait a few minutes and try again, then tell the CSQAU lead.

The QR code will not scan

Type the link instead (page 9). Hold the phone steady, in good light, about 20 cm from the code.

The page will not load

Try again. The first load after a quiet period can be slow. Wait a few seconds and refresh.

Technical appendix
PartDetails
HostingVercel, project nrs-service-surveys. Production deploys from the main branch of https://github.com/DakolMasiyer/NRS-Survey-Form2--8-July.
ApplicationNext.js and TypeScript.
